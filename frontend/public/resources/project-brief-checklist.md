# Your project brief
A practical checklist from SiteGenIT.

## 1. The starting point
- What does your business or product do?
- What problem would you like this project to solve?
- Why now?

## 2. The people
- Who is the primary audience?
- What are their goals and challenges?
- What languages, devices, and accessibility needs matter?

## 3. The experience
- What are the three most important tasks people need to complete?
- Which pages, features, and integrations are essential for launch?
- What can wait until a later phase?

## 4. The content
- Who will provide copy, images, and brand assets?
- Who owns the rights to those assets?
- Does anything need to move from an existing website?

## 5. The collaboration
- Who is the decision maker?
- Who will provide feedback, and how often?
- What timeline and budget range should guide the proposal?

## 6. The outcome
- How will you measure success?
- Who will maintain content and manage accounts after launch?
- What hosting, training, and ongoing support will you need?
